Frequently Asked Questions

Product Authenticity

Are Velvet Scent products authentic?
Absolutely. Every fragrance sold by Velvet Scent is 100% authentic. We formulate our own signature blends using premium imported ingredients from Grasse (France), the Middle East, and Madagascar. Our products are tightly quality-controlled and sealed in our bespoke packaging.

Shipping & Delivery

How long does delivery take?
For orders within Accra, we offer same-day or next-day delivery. For other regions in Ghana, delivery typically takes 2-3 business days. International shipping via DHL takes 5-7 business days depending on customs clearance in your region.

How much is delivery?
Delivery fees are calculated based on your location and will be discussed when confirming your order via our WhatsApp concierge service.

Returns & Exchanges

Can I return a fragrance if I don't like it?
Due to the nature of our products, we only accept returns for items that are unopened, sealed, and in their original packaging within 14 days of receipt. If an item arrives damaged, please contact our concierge team immediately for a replacement.

Placing Orders

How does the WhatsApp order process work?
We pride ourselves on personalized service. When you build your cart and click "Order on WhatsApp", your cart details are sent directly to our luxury concierge team. A representative will confirm your items, process your payment securely, and arrange your delivery seamlessly.
